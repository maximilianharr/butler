"Do not imagine yourself to have what you have not; but take full account of the excellences which you do possess, and in gratitude remember how you would hanker after them, if you had them not. At the same time take care that in thus hugging them, you do not get into the habit of prizing them so much, that without them you would be perturbed."
Marcus Aurelius' Meditations (Book 7, Verse 27)
