# Common Lawn and Pasture Grasses

These grasses are frequently cultivated for lawns, pastures, and forage across Central Europe.

- Kentucky Bluegrass (Poa pratensis)
  A popular, durable lawn and pasture grass with excellent cold hardiness. It is native to almost all of Europe.

- Tall Fescue (Festuca arundinacea)
  Known for its durability and resistance to heat and drought, it's used for both lawns and as a pasture grass.

- Perennial Ryegrass (Lolium perenne)
  A fast-germinating grass often included in lawn seed mixes for its quick greening and durability.

- Cock's-foot / Orchard Grass (Dactylis glomerata)
  A common hay and forage grass with a distinctive, tufted flowerhead.

- Creeping Red Fescue (Festuca rubra)
  A fine-leafed grass that tolerates shade and is often mixed with other grasses for a dense lawn.

- Chewings Fescue (Festuca rubra subsp. commutata)
  A bunch-type fine fescue that thrives in shady and dry conditions.

- Hard Fescue (Festuca trachyphylla)
  A low-maintenance, drought-tolerant fine fescue with a high resistance to winter freezes.

- Bermuda Grass (Cynodon dactylon)
  A tough, fast-growing grass native to Europe, known for its resilience on sports fields.

- Common Bent (Agrostis capillaris)
  Often found in pastures and lawns, it forms a dense turf.

- Timothy-grass (Phleum pratense)
  A major forage grass, widely grown for hay.

- Meadow Foxtail (Alopecurus pratensis)
  An early-flowering pasture grass common in meadows.

- Smooth Meadow-grass (Poa pratensis)
  Another name for Kentucky Bluegrass, valued in pastures.

# Native and Wild Grasses

These species are integral to Central Europe's natural and semi-natural grasslands, including the steppes and meadows.

- Big Bluestem (Andropogon gerardii)
  A tall prairie grass with blue-green foliage that turns orange in the fall.

- Little Bluestem (Schizachyrium scoparium)
  A native grass with blue-green clumps and a fluffy white seed plume, also with attractive fall color.

- Indian Grass (Sorghastrum nutans)
  A tall grass native to prairie regions, with tan-yellow seed heads.

- Switch Grass (Panicum virgatum)
  An upright grass with airy flowers, tolerant of various soil conditions.

- Feather Reed Grass (Calamagrostis epigejos)
  A tall, reed-like grass often found in a variety of habitats.

- Tufted Hairgrass (Deschampsia cespitosa)
  Found in a range of environments from meadows to woodlands, with airy flower panicles.

- Common Reed (Phragmites australis)
  A very tall wetland grass that forms dense stands.

- June Grass (Koeleria macrantha)
  A small, native clump grass with showy white flower panicles in June.

- Side-oats Grama (Bouteloua curtipendula)
  A prairie grass recognized by its one-sided seed head arrangement.

- Blue Grama (Bouteloua gracilis)
  A short, hardy native grass that is highly drought-tolerant.

- Sand Love Grass (Eragrostis trichodes)
  A native grass with pink flowers that thrives in sandy soil.

- Blue Oat Grass (Helictotrichon sempervirens)
  An ornamental grass from central Europe with dense, blue, pointed leaves.

- Needlegrass (Stipa krylovii)
  A dominant grass of the Eurasian steppe.

- Lessing Feather Grass (Stipa lessingiana)
  Another characteristic grass of the steppe ecosystem.

- Indian Rice Grass (Oryzopsis hymenoides)
  A tufted native grass with airy flower panicles, often found in dry, sandy soils.

- Northern Sea Oats (Chasmanthium latifolium)
  A shade-tolerant grass with distinctive, drooping flowers.

- Sheep's Fescue (Festuca ovina)
  A fine-leaved, densely tufted grass common in dry, infertile soils.

- Sweet Vernal Grass (Anthoxanthum odoratum)
  Known for its sweet scent, reminiscent of new-mown hay.
